# Community Onboarding Template (Discord / New Members)

A welcome + orientation flow for new members around a `{game}` launch.

## Variables
- `{game}` — e.g., Grand Theft Auto VI
- `{community_name}` — your server/community name
- `{rules_channel}` — e.g., #rules
- `{news_channel}` — e.g., #news
- `{cta}` — first action for new members (react for a role, say hi, etc.)

## Welcome Message

Welcome to {community_name} — your hub for everything {game}.

Start here:
- Read {rules_channel} so we keep things friendly and IP-safe.
- Turn on alerts in {news_channel} for confirmed news only.
- {cta}

## Roles / Reactions
- 🟦 PS5   🟩 Xbox   🟨 PC — react to get your platform role.

## IP Safety
- Share official links and clips only.
- No leaks, datamined content, or re-hosted copyrighted art.

## Notes
- Keep onboarding to three steps or fewer; add depth in pinned posts.
