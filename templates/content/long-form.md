# Long-Form Video Template (Game Launch)

A skeleton for 8-20 minute launch guides, explainers, or deep dives.

## Variables
- `{game}` — e.g., Grand Theft Auto VI
- `{release_date}` — e.g., TBD or confirmed date
- `{platforms}` — e.g., PS5, Xbox Series X|S
- `{topic}` — the single focus of the video
- `{cta}` — subscribe, join Discord, or visit landing page

## Structure

1. **Cold open (0:00-0:20)**
   - State the payoff: what the viewer will know or be able to do by the end.
   - Reference `{game}` and `{topic}` clearly.

2. **Context (0:20-1:30)**
   - Why this matters for `{game}` right now (`{release_date}`, `{platforms}`).
   - Confirmed facts only; label any rumor explicitly.

3. **Body (segmented)**
   - 3-5 chaptered segments, each one takeaway.
   - Show, don't just tell: use footage of the action being described.

4. **Recap + CTA (final 30s)**
   - Summarize the takeaways in one line.
   - One clear `{cta}` verbally and on-screen.

## Notes
- Add chapter markers so the video is skimmable.
- Keep claims sourced; never present speculation as confirmed.
