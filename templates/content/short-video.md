# Short Video Template (Game Launch)

A skeleton for 30–90 second launch-focused shorts.

## Variables
- `{game}` — e.g., Grand Theft Auto VI
- `{release_date}` — e.g., 19 November 2026
- `{platforms}` — e.g., PS5, Xbox Series X|S
- `{hook}` — 3–6 word high-impact phrase
- `{cta}` — subscribe, join Discord, or visit landing page

## Structure

1. **Hook (0–3s)**
   - Bold statement about `{game}` or `{release_date}`.
   - Visual countdown or "CONFIRMED" stamp for news.

2. **Value (3–45s)**
   - One focused topic per short: setup tip, performance tweak, early money route, or news explainer.
   - Use confirmed facts and clearly label rumors.

3. **CTA (45–90s)**
   - Invite viewers to a hub: Discord, email list, or long-form guide.
   - Mention `{cta}` once verbally and once on-screen.

## Notes

- Keep text readable on mobile; avoid dense overlays.
- Test different `{hook}` variants and log performance for reuse.
