# Email Update Template (Launch Alert)

A short broadcast email for launch news or a content drop.

## Variables
- `{game}` — e.g., Grand Theft Auto VI
- `{news_type}` — trailer, release date, feature reveal
- `{summary}` — 1-2 sentences of what changed
- `{link}` — where to go next (video, landing page, Discord)
- `{cta}` — the single action you want readers to take

## Email

**Subject:** {game}: {news_type} is here

Hey there,

{summary}

See it here: {link}

{cta}

Talk soon,
The team

---

## Notes
- One idea, one CTA. Keep it under ~120 words.
- Include an unsubscribe link and comply with local email rules.
