# Product Bundle Template (Digital Launch Bundle)

A structure for packaging guides, checklists, and overlays into a sellable bundle.

## Variables
- `{game}` — e.g., Grand Theft Auto VI
- `{bundle_name}` — e.g., "Launch Day Survival Kit"
- `{price}` — e.g., $12
- `{link}` — checkout or storefront link
- `{cta}` — the action (get the bundle, pre-order, etc.)

## Listing

**{bundle_name} — for {game}**

What's inside:
- A launch-week progression checklist.
- A settings/performance guide.
- Stream overlays and starter graphics (original assets only).

Price: {price}

{cta}: {link}

## Notes
- Only include original or properly licensed assets — no Rockstar IP.
- Bundle items should each stand alone but be more valuable together.
