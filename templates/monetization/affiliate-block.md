# Affiliate Block Template (Gear / Software)

A reusable block for video descriptions, blog posts, and landing pages.

## Variables
- `{game}` — e.g., Grand Theft Auto VI
- `{category}` — e.g., console, storage, capture card, microphone, editing software
- `{product_name}` — display name of the product
- `{affiliate_link}` — your tracked link
- `{one_liner}` — why it matters for this launch

## Block

**My {game} setup — {category}**

- **{product_name}** — {one_liner}
  - Get it: {affiliate_link}

_Disclosure: some links are affiliate links. I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you._

## Notes
- Keep disclosures clear and compliant with local advertising rules.
- Only recommend gear you have used or genuinely stand behind.
- Reuse the same block across shorts, long-form, and landing pages by swapping variables.
