# Creator Toolbox

A modular toolkit for building and operating creator-economy launch systems around major games and media events, starting with Grand Theft Auto VI.

## Vision

Creator Toolbox is a repo for **repeatable launch infrastructure**: content pipelines, monetization frameworks, community playbooks, and orchestration configs that you can reuse across different releases.

The initial focus is GTA 6, which is expected to be one of the largest creator moments in gaming history, with huge demand for guides, streams, tools, and launch-day workflows.[web:24][web:45]

## Core Ideas

- **Cookie-cutter, game-agnostic templates** you can parameterize (e.g., `{game}`, `{release_date}`, `{platforms}`, `{affiliate_links}`) and reuse for future launches.
- **Orchestrator-first design**: treat content, community, and monetization as pipelines that agents can coordinate end-to-end.
- **IP-safe creator tooling**: stay aligned with official terms of use and avoid relying on leaks, unconfirmed mechanics, or copyrighted assets.[web:45]
- **Monetization playbooks**: structured ways to earn from content, products, and services (not just ad revenue), inspired by current creator-economy and AI-content best practices.[web:37][web:40]

## Repo Layout (early scaffold)

- `docs/` — architecture notes, playbooks, and checklists.
- `templates/` — reusable content and community templates (shorts, long-form, Discord, email, landing pages).
- `orchestrator/` — future home for agent configs, workflows, and scripts.

This is an early scaffold. Expect breaking changes while the architecture matures.
