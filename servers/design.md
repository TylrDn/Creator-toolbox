# RP server concept

Working design doc for a GTA/FiveM-style RP server business. Adjust factions, jobs, and monetization to match platform rules when they are published.

## Value proposition

A moderated RP server with clear progression, stable economy, and content that produces shareable clips. Players pay for convenience and identity (whitelist priority, cosmetics), not pay-to-win power.

## Jobs

- Entry jobs: delivery, taxi, mechanic trainee (low payout, low friction)
- Skilled jobs: EMS, police trainee, legal trades (training RP required)
- Faction-linked roles: unlocked through application and playtime

## Factions

- Civilian businesses (restaurants, shops) as player-run fronts
- Emergency services with strict SOP docs
- Criminal orgs with approval-based entry and heat mechanics

Keep faction count small at launch; add branches after moderation capacity grows.

## Economy

- Sinks: rent, fines, repairs, licenses
- Faucets: jobs, legal businesses, event payouts
- Admin tooling to tune balances weekly from transaction logs

## Progression

- Playtime and job rank unlocks (vehicles, housing tiers)
- Reputation per faction; resets on rule violations
- Seasonal goals to re-engage lapsed players

## Moderation needs

- Written rules + strike system
- Active staff coverage for peak hours
- Logging for reports, bans, and economy anomalies
- Clear spoiler and meta-gaming policies

## Monetization ideas (hypothesis)

- Subscription: queue priority, cosmetic packs, supporter badge
- One-time: whitelist application fee (if allowed by platform)
- Avoid selling in-game currency or weapons directly

## Risks

- Staff burnout and inconsistent enforcement
- Economy exploits from third-party scripts
- Platform ToS changes affecting monetization
