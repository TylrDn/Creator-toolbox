# Subscription tiers (hypothesis)

Monetization model for RP server — **cosmetic and convenience only**, no pay-to-win. Verify against platform terms before launch.

## Free

- Public queue
- Standard character slots
- Community events access

## Supporter ($5–10/mo)

- Queue priority (cap wait reduction, not skip-all)
- Supporter Discord role + badge
- Early event RSVP

## Premium ($15–25/mo)

- Cosmetic vehicle skins (server-approved list)
- Extra character slot
- Monthly "office hours" with admins

## One-time

- Whitelist application fee (optional, if allowed)
- Name reservation for launch window

## Rules

- No selling in-game cash, weapons, or rank
- Refund policy: 7-day window for first subscription charge
- Chargebacks = permanent ban

## Ops checklist

- [ ] Payment provider linked (Tebex, Patreon, or Stripe — your choice)
- [ ] Role sync to Discord documented
- [ ] Tier perks listed in `#rules` and website
- [ ] Staff trained on refund/appeal flow

## KPIs

- Monthly recurring revenue
- Churn rate per tier
- Queue time delta (free vs supporter) — keep fair
